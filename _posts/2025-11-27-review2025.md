Often I think that I need to write more in this blog. And it mostly stays this way – just thoughts. But sometimes (as I can see from amount and regularity of my posts — not often) I actually **do the writing**!
And I do have something to write down. To understend better myself. And to leave my thoughts for review for future self.

So... This year is not over yet, it's end of November still, but it feels over already, becase this time there will be longer Chritsmas and New Years holidays, so not much time left for **new projects**. And time to look back at **current** and **completed projects** – what went well? What didn't? How to do better next year. And where my focus will be.
